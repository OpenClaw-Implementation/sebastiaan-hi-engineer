Hi-Engineer · plan from 12 October 2026

From a working engine to a weekly habit

The data engine for the EPCM Guide is built and live. The next ten weeks turn it into a steady weekly routine: fresh data every Monday, a short review mid-week, clean exports to Airtable every Friday, and a small, predictable budget. By 18 December the Netherlands directory should be clean, enriched and flowing into the portal.

Pogan · build & run Seb · direction & review Together

What makes the next phase easier

  • Everything runs from one web app; no local tools needed to review data.
  • Re-importing the master Excel is safe to repeat, so Seb's manual research flows straight in.
  • CSV and Airtable exports already work for every table.
  • Every paid call is logged with its cost, so spend is visible week by week.

The three gaps this plan closes

  • No schedule: nothing has run automatically since 16 July.
  • Untagged data: 37% of companies have no category; most jobs and news have no tag.
  • Missing emails: 777 of 791 contacts have no email address.
1Who does what

Two roles, one rhythm

Pogan keeps the engine running and improving. Seb sets direction, judges what is relevant, and owns the decisions about tools, portal and spend.

Pogan

Builds and runs the engine

About 3 hours a week, plus project tasks from the timeline
  • Keeps the schedule running and checks every Monday's runs
  • Turns Seb's relevance marks into better tagging rules
  • Runs the integrations within the agreed budget
  • Tests every change before and after it goes live
  • Sends the Friday export to Airtable and a one-line status
Seb

Sets direction and judges quality

About 1.5 hours a week, plus decisions from the timeline
  • Reviews new data mid-week and marks what is relevant
  • Chooses the contact tool, the portal route and the tags
  • Approves the monthly budget and any spend above it
  • Adds manual research (contacts, manufacturers) to the master Excel
  • Uses the data: outreach lists, portal content, partner talks
i

Together, 30 minutes on Thursday: look at the week's numbers, make the decisions that are due, and agree the single most important thing for next week.

Spending rules

  • Any single run expected to cost more than $10 needs a yes from Seb first.
  • Pilots run on a small sample and are capped before they start.
  • If a month reaches 80% of a cap, Pogan pauses that integration and raises it at the Thursday sync.
9Testing

Confidence at every layer

Four kinds of checks, from automatic to human. Together they catch problems before the portal or an outreach list ever sees them.

Pogan

After every deploy

Smoke test: every page loads, counts and filters correct, exports complete. Then a look at desktop and phone.

Pogan

Every Monday

All runs finished; row counts never drop; no duplicate contacts; spend under cap.

Seb

Every Wednesday

20-row spot check and 10-row tag check. Problems go into the Thursday sync.

Together

Every pilot

Same 50 contacts through each tool; compare hit rate, accuracy and cost per email before choosing.
